01 / The idea

The city has food.
The gates have people.
The gap is coordination.

OotaPass starts with families waiting outside Bengaluru’s government hospitals—and plans backwards from when they need dinner.

Gate requests, surplus donations and NGO runners become one feasible plan. When a gate is still short, it says exactly what is missing.

Built by Team C1CORE · BMS Institute of Technology and Management · Rewired 2026

Not just “we have food.”

03 / Trust travels too

A passport
for every batch.

Open a trip to see its food source, runner, declared deadline and QR label. The NGO records a pickup acceptance or rejection; rejected food is removed and the night is re-planned.

OotaPass records decisions. It does not certify food as safe.

“Twenty short”
isn’t enough.
